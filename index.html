Scope 01

Air Conditioning Services

Complete cooling service designed for harsh Texas summers. We address critical mechanical bottlenecks across compressors, evaporator coils, refrigerant lines, and electronic management controls.

Documented Work Undertaken:

  • System Installation & Replacement: Full matched system sizing and replacement for aging or inefficient units.
  • Mechanical Repairs: Fan motors, capacitors, contactors, electrical wiring, and refrigerant leak diagnostics.
  • Preventive Maintenance: Comprehensive preseason tune-ups, pressure verifications, and electrical testing.
  • Coil & Blower Cleaning: Removing accumulated sludge and grime from evaporator coils and blower fans to restore static pressure.
  • Controls: Programmable and digital thermostat installation and calibration.

Common Signs Service Is Needed:

Warm air from supply vents, condenser continuously short-cycling, iced refrigeration lines, unusual grinding or humming noises, or sudden spikes in electrical utility bills.

Scope 02

Heating & Furnace Systems

When Gulf temperatures plummet, reliable heating protects your household pipes and comfort. We provide detailed heating diagnostics focusing on combustion safety, heat exchanger integrity, and electrical reliability.

Documented Work Undertaken:

  • Furnace Repair & Replacement: Gas and electric furnace troubleshooting, burner cleaning, and replacements.
  • Heat Pump Services: Diagnostic check on reversing valves, defrost boards, and auxiliary electric heat strips.
  • Furnace Cleaning & Inspections: Safety evaluations of heat exchangers, draft diverters, and flue venting to prevent carbon monoxide hazards.
  • Thermostat Integration: Heating cycle calibration and zone controls.

Common Signs Service Is Needed:

Furnace blows cool air, persistent burning odors after initial startup, burner ignition failure or repeated clicking, yellow burner flames instead of blue, or safety switch trips.

Scope 03

Indoor Air Quality & Airflow Management

Modern Texas homes are sealed tightly for efficiency, which can trap allergens, volatile organic compounds, dust, and humidity indoors. We analyze and treat the full circulating air stream.

Documented Work Undertaken:

  • Air Quality Assessments: Evaluating duct cleanliness, static pressure restriction, and particulate circulation.
  • Air Purification Systems: Integrating whole-house air purifiers that work alongside your central system.
  • High-Efficiency Filtration: Proper media filter sizing and replacement to stop dust without choking system airflow.
  • Duct & Vent Cleaning: Clearing accumulated dust from branch runs and return plenums.
  • Dryer Vent Cleaning: Clearing lint obstruction in dryer duct pathways to restore airflow and mitigate fire hazards.
Scope 04

Water Heater Installation & Repair

Consistent domestic hot water is a foundational utility. D & T handles the physical repair, routine maintenance, and complete replacement of electric and gas water heating tanks.

Documented Work Undertaken:

  • Electric Water Heaters: Heating element replacement, thermostat checks, and wiring repair.
  • Gas Water Heaters: Thermocouple replacement, burner servicing, gas control valve diagnostics, and venting.
  • Preventive Maintenance: Flushing mineral sediment to extend tank longevity and heat-transfer efficiency.
  • Tank Replacement: Professional removal and clean replacement of corroded or leaking tanks.
